Hybrid and livestream production

Many event crews treat the stream as an add-on. IDW plans it from the first call, using the broadcast workflows Isaac works with every day in a broadcast facility, so remote viewers get a real show rather than a webcam pointed at a stage.

In the room

  • FOH mix built for the audience in front of you
  • Wireless mics and IEMs coordinated and monitored
  • Confidence monitors, playback, and walk-ins on cue

On the stream

  • A separate broadcast mix, not a copy of the room
  • Multicamera switching with remote PTZ cameras
  • Remote presenters on Zoom or Teams with no echo
  • Program recording handed off after the show
